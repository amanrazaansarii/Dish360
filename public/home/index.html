    Quick answers.

    Do guests need an app?

    No. They scan the table QR with their phone camera and the menu opens in the browser, on iPhone and Android.

    How do you make the 3D dishes?

    From a normal phone photo of each plated dish. You approve every dish before guests see it.

    What if a price changes or a dish sells out?

    Update it in your dashboard. Every table's QR shows the change straight away, so nothing is reprinted.

    How it works, from your kitchen to their table

    Five steps. Follow one dish all the way through.

    1. 1You

      Take a photo of each dish

      Use your phone. Put the dish on a plain table in good light and take one clear photo. Send us the photos along with your menu.

      That is all the preparation you need. No special camera, no studio.

    2. 2We

      We turn each photo into 3D

      Our system builds a 3D model of the dish from your photo: first its shape, then its colours. It looks like the real plate, from every side.

      You check every dish and approve it before any guest sees it.

    3. 3You

      Put a code on every table

      We give you a QR code for each table, ready to print on a small stand. It is the only thing your guests need to find the menu.

      The code never changes, even when your menu does.

    4. 4Your guest

      Your guest scans and sees the dish

      They point their phone camera at the code. The menu opens in the web browser, no app to download. They tap a dish and it appears on the table in front of them, at real size.

      What is AR?Augmented reality (AR) shows a 3D object through the phone's camera, as if it were really sitting on the table.
    5. 5You

      Change anything, any time

      Update a price, add a dish, or mark one as sold out from your dashboard. Every table shows the change straight away. There is nothing to reprint.

      You can also see which dishes your guests look at most. Try the controls in the picture.

    Questions owners ask

    Do my guests need to download an app?

    No. The menu opens in the phone's web browser as soon as they scan the code.

    Does it work on iPhone and Android?

    Yes. It opens in the browser on both, and the dish is shown with the phone's own AR viewer.

    What happens when a dish changes or sells out?

    Change it in your dashboard. Every table's code shows the update straight away, so you never reprint anything.

    Do I need to know anything about 3D?

    No. You send photos, we make the 3D models, and you approve them before guests see them.
